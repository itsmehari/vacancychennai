# Chennai GTM — 90-day checklist

Use alongside `docs/EMPLOYER_BILLING_POLICY.md` and the live `/pricing` page.

## Partnerships (targets)

- Training institutes (IT, accounting, nursing) — co-branded “first post” packs.
- Staffing agencies — referral fee or bulk volume SKUs.
- Colleges / placement cells — fresher bundles + campus QR to `/employer/register`.

## WhatsApp (templates — adapt tone)

1. **Cold HR intro:** “We list Chennai roles by area on vacancychennai.in — moderated, ₹75 single post or ₹49/post on volume. Can I send the employer signup link?”
2. **Follow-up:** “Here’s pricing: [link to /pricing]. Happy to add your areas if something’s missing.”

## Paid tests (small budget)

- Google Search: intent keywords (`jobs in ambattur`, `chennai hiring manufacturing`).
- Meta: geo Chennai + OMR / Sriperumbudur radii; landing `/post-job` or `/pricing`.

## Organic / SEO

- Keep `/jobs-in-*` area pages fresh; internal link from each hub to `/pricing` for employers.
- Google Business Profile for the brand; link to `/jobs-in-chennai`.

## Offline micro-tests

- Print QR → `/employer/register` + copy “First post ₹75” (match live SKU in `src/lib/billing/skus.ts`).
- Test corridors: Ambattur industrial belt, OMR tech stretch, Sriperumbudur logistics pockets.

## Funnel metrics (weekly)

Track: visits → employer signup → verified email → checkout started → payment succeeded → first published job → applications.
