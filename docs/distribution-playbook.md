# Vacancy Chennai Distribution Playbook

## 1. Daily Channel Plan
- WhatsApp groups:
  - Jobs in OMR
  - Jobs in Tambaram
  - Jobs in Ambattur
  - Jobs in Porur
- Telegram:
  - Vacancy Chennai main channel

## 2. Daily Quotas
- Jobs seeded: 15-25
- Jobs moderated/published: 10-20
- WhatsApp posts: 10-20
- Telegram posts: all newly published jobs
- Employer outreach calls: 20+

## 3. Posting Format
Template:
1. Job title
2. Area + landmark
3. Salary range
4. Job type
5. Apply link

Example:
- Delivery Associate - Tambaram (Near Railway Station)
- Salary: INR 15,000-22,000
- Type: Part-time
- Apply: https://vacancychennai.in/jobs/job-002

## 4. Moderation Rules Before Distribution
- Title is clear and non-misleading
- Salary is present and realistic
- Location mapped to known area
- Contact details are valid
- No duplicate repost within 48h unless status changed

## 5. Weekly Reporting
- Published jobs by zone
- Applications per zone
- Time-to-first-applicant by category
- Top source channel (WhatsApp/Telegram/Organic SEO)
